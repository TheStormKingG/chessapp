# Unit 1.2 — Capturing and value

## The habit

**Take hanging pieces, never hang your own.**

A piece is **hanging** when it is attacked and nothing defends it. Taking one
costs you nothing, and leaving one of yours out there gives the game away.

## What the pieces are worth

| Piece  | Value |
| ------ | ----- |
| Pawn   | 1     |
| Knight | 3     |
| Bishop | 3     |
| Rook   | 5     |
| Queen  | 9     |

The king has no number: you can never lose him, so he is never traded.

## Counting on a square

1. Count every piece of yours that **attacks** the square.
2. Count every enemy piece that **defends** it.
3. If your attackers outnumber the defenders, you can win the square.
4. Take with your **cheapest** piece first, so the least is at risk.

Three attackers against one defender means take. One attacker against two
defenders means leave it alone.

## Before every move

Ask two questions, in this order:

- Is anything of theirs free right now?
- After my move, is anything of mine free?

That second question is the one people forget.
